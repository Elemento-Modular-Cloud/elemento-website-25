Under the Hood

How Electros works behind the scenes

Electros is a desktop app backed by core services for hybrid and multicloud work. Atomosphere translates CSP and private-cloud APIs into one Elemento protocol so Electros can provision, compare costs, and operate workloads without you switching consoles.

Technical Architecture:

  • Shared core services underpin the desktop app
  • Atomosphere API proxy over private clouds and CSP accounts
  • Automatic protocol selection based on guest OS
  • Unified workflows for VMs, storage, and networking
  • Provider-agnostic provisioning and cost comparison

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Electros functionality

How do I get started?

If you do not have an Elemento account yet, create one first — you get an organisation and a 14-day free trial. If you already have an account, open Buy a seat to purchase for an organisation via Stripe.

Where do I pay?

On the Buy a seat page, sign in, choose an organisation and plan, then Stripe opens in your browser. Subscribe during the trial so you do not lose access when it ends.

Does Electros run offline?

Yes, basic inventory and local VM management work without network, syncing when online.

Which operating systems are supported?

Native desktop apps for Linux, macOS, and Windows. Get AppImage, DMG, EXE, and .deb packages from electros.cloud.

What access protocols are supported?

SSH (Linux/BSD), RDP/VNC (Windows/Linux GUI). 4K streaming is coming soon.

Is Electros free to use?

New accounts get a 14-day free trial once. After that, each organisation needs an Electros seat subscription — one seat per organisation. Sign in above to buy a seat; checkout opens in Stripe.
