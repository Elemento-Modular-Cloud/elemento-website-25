How to get the most out of SaaS Black Friday deals

The best deal is a tool you would have bought anyway, at a price you lock in for longer. A few checks before you hit "buy":

  • Check the renewal price. Many discounts apply only to the first year or the first 12 months. Know what you'll pay after that.
  • Go annual or lifetime only for tools you use every week. For experiments, a discounted monthly plan is the safer bet.
  • Ask where your data goes. With AI tools especially, check where prompts, documents and code are processed, and who can read them.
  • Note the end dates. Most of the deals below close between December 2 and December 31.
